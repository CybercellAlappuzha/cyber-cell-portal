Cyber Cell Request Proforma

Fill in, generate the PDF, print and send to the Cyber Cell

Nothing you enter here is saved or sent anywhere — the PDF is created in your own browser. Fill the form, press Generate PDF, then print it and route it to the Cyber Cell the usual way.

Case particulars
Subscriber / user details *

One row per number, IMEI, or Aadhaar number.

Required details *

Tick everything that is being requested. Each one you tick produces its own separate PDF proforma — a station ticking CDR, IMEI Trace and Aadhaar search gets 3 separate PDFs to print and send. Address is the exception: tick it alongside CDR, CAF, or Certified copy and it merges into that PDF instead of making a separate one — it only gets its own PDF when none of those three are ticked. Address doesn't apply to IMEI Trace, so it's turned off and disabled while IMEI Trace is ticked.

Justification & remarks

The PDF ends with the office's standard Declaration text and a "Verified by ISHO / Recommended by SDPO" signature box, left blank for them to sign and seal by hand after printing.
