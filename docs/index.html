Case particulars
Required details *

Tick everything that is being requested. Each one you tick produces its own separate PDF proforma — a station ticking CDR and Aadhaar search gets 2 separate PDFs to print and send, plus a 3rd if IMEI Trace is also ticked in its own box below. Address is the exception: tick it alongside CDR, CAF, or Certified copy and it merges into that PDF instead of making a separate one — it only gets its own PDF when none of those three are ticked.
